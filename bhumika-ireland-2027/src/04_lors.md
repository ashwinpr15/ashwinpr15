# Deliverable 4 — Three Letters of Recommendation

<div class="callout warn">
<strong>How to use these drafts (read first).</strong> Each letter is a <em>draft for the referee to review, edit and adopt as their own</em>. Send it to the referee together with her CV and SOP. Every fact in square brackets must be filled or confirmed by the referee: name, title, how long they have known her, course names, project title. Irish portals (Galway, UL, DCU, Trinity) usually <strong>email the referee directly</strong> and ask them to upload a signed letter on institutional letterhead from an official email address, so referees should expect that email. A letter must never be signed or submitted on a referee's behalf. The three letters are deliberately written in different voices; please don't merge them into one template.
</div>
